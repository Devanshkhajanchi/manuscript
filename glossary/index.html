Glossary

Banality

ba·nal·i·ty
/bəˈnalədē,bāˈnalədē/

Noun

The fact or condition of being banal; unoriginality.

"There is an essential banality to the story he tells"

Criticism

crit·i·cism
/ˈkridəˌsizəm/

Noun

The expression of disapproval of someone or something based on perceived faults or mistakes.

"He received a lot of criticism"

Noun

The analysis and judgment of the merits and faults of a literary or artistic work.

"Alternative methods of criticism supported by well-developed literary theories"

Critique

cri·tique
/krəˈtēk/

Noun

A detailed analysis and assessment of something, especially a literary, philosophical, or political theory.

"A critique of Marxist historicism"

Verb

Evaluate (a theory or practice) in a detailed and analytical way.

"The authors critique the methods and practices used in the research"
